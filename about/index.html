About

Car club and gaming trailer.

Team Battle Ready is Joe Bonilla’s car club in Central Texas, and a gaming trailer for birthdays, BBQs, and game nights around Killeen and Fort Hood.

The club and the trailer

The trailer is a mobile game room with 6 dedicated gaming stations. A game coach stays on site for the whole booking, and Wi-Fi is available for online multiplayer. People book it for birthdays, BBQs, game nights, and family days. The club is meets and cruises. Booking the trailer does not sign you up for the club. Following the club does not reserve the trailer.

The gaming trailer is still under construction. Rental availability is limited until it is complete. Climate control (A/C and heat) is being installed as part of the build. It is not live yet.

Youth events

Youth events are planned with a second adult on site.
