Harker Heights, Texas

The game trailer, parked in Harker Heights.

Harker Heights sits next to Killeen and is one of the four usual cities. Travel is free. Skirmish starts at $349.

The gaming trailer is still under construction. Rental availability is limited until it is complete. Climate control (A/C and heat) is being installed as part of the build. It is not live yet.

Starts at $349

Skirmish is 2 hours at $349. Mission is 3 hours at $449. Campaign is 4 hours at $549. The price is the same every day. Prices exclude tax. The quote adds 8.25%. The deposit is a flat $100 after we confirm the day.

What people book in Harker Heights

  • Birthdays at the house.
  • Schools and churches, at a place the trailer can legally park.
  • Military family days, homecomings, and unit days at a house or driveway.

Birthdays and family days are the usual ask: a house with a driveway or side street long enough for the truck and trailer, on level ground.

Schools and churches book the same way. The spot still has to be a place the trailer can legally park.

Fort Hood is a different trip. A Heights address is free travel. A Fort Hood address is by exception, quoted by text, and a 4-hour minimum may apply.

Included

Six stations with 27-inch monitors and consoles, 8 stand-alone 8-inch tablets, a game coach on site the whole booking, Wi-Fi for online multiplayer, and a self-powered 13.5 kW generator. No outlet needed. Setup and teardown are included.
