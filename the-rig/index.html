Inside the trailer

What’s on the trailer.

Six dedicated stations with 27-inch monitors, the consoles, the tablets, Wi-Fi, and an on-site attendant. Power on board is a 13.5 kW generator. No outlet needed. The shell is unwrapped. Magnets or a banner are fine.The gaming trailer is still under construction. Rental availability is limited until it is complete. Climate control (A/C and heat) is being installed as part of the build. It is not live yet.

What is on board

  • 6 dedicated gaming stations. Each has a 27-inch gaming monitor, a gaming chair, and a console: Xbox Series S/X, PS5, or Nintendo Switch.
  • Certain games are 4-player multiplayer.
  • About 4 Xbox (Series S/X), 3 PlayStation 5 consoles, and various games.
  • 8 stand-alone 8-inch tablets for younger kids. Roblox and Minecraft style play.
  • A game coach stays on site for the whole booking. The host sets the game-rating limit. Setup and teardown are included.
  • Wi-Fi is available for online multiplayer.
  • A self-powered 13.5 kW generator. No household outlet needed.
  • The trailer is stealth and unwrapped. Magnets or a banner are fine. It does not wear a full wrap.
  • Food, drinks, and gum stay outside.

Parking

The truck and trailer need a driveway or a side street where they can legally park for the booking. Confirm that spot before the date. If it will not work, call or text and we will figure out another option. Plan on about 50–60 feet of level space until the real footprint is measured.
