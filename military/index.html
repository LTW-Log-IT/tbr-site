Fort Hood

Thank you for your service.

Discounts available for active duty and veterans. Military, first responders, and teachers get $25 off the package price, or off the organization hourly total, with an ID. It is a flat $25, once. Discounts do not stack. Travel and add-ons stay full price. Square charges the full package price. Call or text to apply the $25. Skirmish is $349 − $25 = $324.

The same packages cover a birthday, a BBQ, a game night, or a family day.

Most bookings are a driveway in Killeen, Copperas Cove, Harker Heights, or Nolanville. Travel there is free. Fort Hood and other towns are outside that area. Those trips are by exception only. They have to be coordinated and approved ahead of time. Prices vary. A minimum of 4 hours may be required.

The room is 6 dedicated gaming stations. Each one has a 27-inch gaming monitor, a gaming chair, and a console: Xbox Series S/X, PS5, or Nintendo Switch. Certain games are 4-player multiplayer. Wi-Fi is available for online multiplayer. A game coach stays on site the whole booking. Also about 4 Xbox, 3 PS5s, and 8 stand-alone 8-inch tablets for younger kids (Roblox and Minecraft), plus various games.

Birthday

A Campaign is four hours at $549, any day. A six-hour full day is $749. An eight-hour full day is $949. The $25 discount applies once. A verified organization can ask about the hourly rate. A home party cannot.

BBQ

A backyard BBQ often fits a Skirmish. A family day or a longer hang usually fits a Mission or a Campaign. Yard games are an add-on. They are not included in the package price.

Game night

Same lengths as a birthday. A tournament pack is an add-on if you want brackets. Book the date when you know it. The trailer meets you at a driveway.
