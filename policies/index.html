Policies

What is set, and what is not.

Deposit, payment, parking, and liability are below. Cancellation, reschedule, and the balance are blank until Joe sets them. Ask by text. This is not a finished legal policy.

Deposit

The deposit is a flat $100 on every booking. Call or text (254) 251-5219 first so we can confirm the day. Then pay the deposit on Square to hold it.

Pay deposit · $100

Prices on the card exclude tax. Quotes add 8.25%.

Square package links

These charge the full package price. The $25 ID discount is not built into the link. Call or text if Square cannot adjust it. Discounts do not stack. Travel and extra time stay full price.

  • Skirmish · $349
  • Mission · $449
  • Campaign · $549

Balance

Not set yet

When the rest of the bill is due is not published. Confirm the balance when you book. Text (254) 251-5219.

Cancellation and reschedule

Not set yet

There is no cancellation window, fee, or credit rule on this site. Ask when you book.

Weather

Not set yet

Weather rules are not published. Ask when you book. Climate control (A/C and heat) is being installed as part of the build. It is not live yet.

Parking

The truck and trailer need a driveway or a side street where they can legally park for the booking, on level space. Confirm that spot before the date. If parking will not work, call or text and we will figure out another option.

Power

The trailer is self-powered with a 13.5 kW generator. No household outlet is needed.

Food and drinks

Food, drinks, and gum stay outside the trailer.

Ratings

The host sets the game-rating limit and tells us when booking. The on-site attendant follows that limit. A parent or guardian is responsible for minors.

Attendant and Wi-Fi

A game coach stays on site for the whole booking. Wi-Fi is available for online multiplayer.

Discounts

Active duty, veterans, first responders, and teachers: $25 off the package price, or off the Monday–Thursday hourly total, with an ID. Discounts do not stack.

Travel

Travel is free in Killeen, Copperas Cove, Harker Heights, and Nolanville. Outside that area, including Fort Hood, trips are by exception only. They have to be approved ahead of time. The trip is quoted by text. Prices vary. A minimum of 4 hours may be required. The town list is on the areas page.

Liability

Assumption of risk. Participation and use of the equipment are at the guest’s own risk. Lead the Way Logistics & IT LLC and Team Battle Ready are not liable for injury, property damage, or loss arising from the rental, except as required by law. A parent or guardian is responsible for minors. Guests follow the house rules and take care of the gear.

The gaming trailer is still under construction. Rental availability is limited until it is complete.
